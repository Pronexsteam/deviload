# Music vinyl asset

`src/assets/music-vinyl.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with a transparent background. The album artwork is a separate, dynamic circular label; it is not baked into the generated image. Both rotate around the same center and pause with playback. OS reduced-motion preferences disable rotation.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a production raster asset for a sophisticated OLED-black desktop music player: one realistic black vinyl record, perfectly circular, dead-center, viewed straight from above with no perspective. Transparent background and transparent outside the disc. The record fills 94% of a square canvas. Fine concentric grooves, glossy charcoal material, clearly visible asymmetrical soft silver highlight arcs so rotation will be perceptible at 48 to 160 pixels. Small warm ivory center label, tiny dark spindle hole, one subtle black radial mark on the label. No text, no letters, no logos, no cover sleeve, no turntable, no hands, no ground shadow, no colored neon, no cartoon look. Premium understated physical material. Exact centered axis for seamless CSS rotation.
